# Privacy Policy for DCI Teachers App

**Last Updated: July 2026**

DCI Teachers ("we", "us", or "our") operates the DCI Teachers App. This policy informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service.

### 1. Information Collection and Use
We collect several different types of information for various purposes to provide and improve our Service to you.
* **Personal Data**: While using our Service, we may ask you to provide us with certain personally identifiable information, such as your Email Address, Full Name, and Phone Number.
* **Student Data**: The app allows teachers to import and manage student information (IDs, Grades, Attendance). This data is stored securely in our private database.

### 2. Data Storage
Your data is stored using Google Firebase. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information.

### 3. Data Sharing
We do not sell or share your personal data or your students' data with any third parties.

### 4. Contact Us
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: admin@dciteachers.com
